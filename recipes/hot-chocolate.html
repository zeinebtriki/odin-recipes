Hot Chocolate Recipe

Description

This classic hot chocolate recipe combines simple ingredients to make an incredibly rich and creamy, comforting drink.

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Vanilla
  • Half-and-half

Steps

  1. Add sugar and cocoa powder with a pinch of salt
  2. Pour boiling water over the sugar, cocoa powder, and salt.
  3. Add whole, 2 percent, or the alternative milk of your choice.
  4. Add a dash of Vanilla
  5. Add Half-and-half after the hot chocolate is poured into the mugs,
