Carrot Salad Recipe

Description

This easy side dish of fresh carrots, sweet raisins, and tangy lemon complements almost any meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound shredded carrots
  • 1 ¼ cups raisins
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise, or more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Mix shredded carrots and raisins together in a large salad bowl.
  2. Whisk mayonnaise, lemon juice, and salt in a small bowl until smooth.
  3. Pour over carrot mixture; stir until carrots and raisins are completely coated.
  4. Refrigerate until chilled and flavors are combined, at least 30 minutes.
